# Public media and avatar references

Research checked October 17, 2026 for the owner's request to create a Wine Concierge host resembling his dad. This research branch stores source links and credits; it does not change the deployed app.

## Primary portrait candidate

WALTER Magazine, **Bottling the Past at Locklear Vineyard and Winery**, by Wiley Cash, photography by Mallory Cash:
https://waltermagazine.com/food/locklear-vineyard/

The feature profiles the elder Charlie Locklear and the family winery. It includes a clear portrait of an older man in a checked winery shirt beside the vines; this is the best portrait reference candidate found. Keep the source attribution with the design notes. Do not identify people by facial matching; the source article and owner's confirmation establish the intended subject.

Portrait URL:
https://waltermagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/4R1A2593-1024x683.jpg

Other article photographs show Love in a Bottle, the winery building, a Noblos label bearing a drawn likeness, and a person walking beside vines with a dog. No original publisher photographs are committed to this repository.

  https://www.ourstate.com/a-wine-lovers-escape-in-eastern-north-carolina/

  https://discoverbrightside.com/bwstudioz

  https://jennisbeveragemarketing.com/2021/10/06/summer-in-a-bottle-locklear/
- Winey Friends, **Charlie T. Locklear Vineyard and Winery**: first-person visitor account of meeting Charlie and tasting wines. Visit date was not established.
  https://www.wineyfriends.com/wineries/charlie-t.-locklear-vineyard-and-winery

## Avatar direction

A welcoming illustrated wine host resembling the elder Charlie, with recognizable facial features and white hair, a checked collared shirt, and restrained burgundy/cream brand accents. Use a head-and-shoulders composition readable as a small chat avatar. This is an illustrated assistant inspired by the founder, not a representation that the founder is personally replying live.
